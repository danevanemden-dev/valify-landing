A valuation that shows its working.

Every comparable sale, every adjustment, on the record — and still signed off by a qualified valuer.

Valify is an early-stage property valuation venture combining AI-assisted analysis with qualified human valuers. We're piloting the model on a defined pocket of Sydney's market — the eastern suburbs — before deciding how far to take it.

The aim is a model that handles most, and eventually hopefully all, of the steps involved in producing a valuation, built up from real comparable-sale evidence rather than a black-box estimate — with a clear audit trail behind every figure.

01 Gather Comparable sales are located and matched against the subject property's location, land size, and dwelling type.
02 Analyse Each comparable is assessed against the evidence — land, building, condition, aspect, outlook — and weighed against the subject.
03 Certify A qualified valuer reviews the analysis and signs off the final figure before it goes out.
